An RO Water Chiller is a specialized refrigeration unit with an internal stainless steel (SS304/SS316) heat exchanger and storage reservoir. It directly connects to the output product water line of an RO purification plant. As the purified water passes through the cooling chamber, high-efficiency refrigeration coils cool it down to your desired temperature (8°C to 18°C) ready for instant dispensing.
In typical climatic conditions with dust and pollutants, we recommend deep power jet cleaning twice a year: once at the beginning of the summer season and once halfway through. Regular jet foam cleaning lowers power consumption by up to 25%, prevents compressor overheating, and avoids costly coil leakages.
Any component replaced by us (such as capacitors, fan motors, sensors, contactors, or copper leak repairs) carries a comprehensive 90-day replacement warranty. If cooling drops or the same problem recurs, our technician will inspect and service it at zero service/visit cost.
